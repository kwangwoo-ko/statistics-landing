About Statistics Tool

Statistics Tool (statistics.thinkflo.net) is a free, browser-based statistical analysis and graphing tool. It was built on one simple belief: basic data analysis and publication-quality graphs should not require an expensive license.

Why We Built This

Millions of researchers, students, and analysts rely on tools like GraphPad Prism to create graphs and run statistical tests. But commercial licenses cost hundreds of dollars per year — a real barrier for students, independent researchers, and labs in developing countries.

We believe the most commonly used features — bar graphs, box plots, violin plots, scatter plots, t-tests, and ANOVA — should be available to everyone, free, right in the browser.

What We Offer

  • 7 graph types — bar, box, violin, scatter, line, histogram, and heatmap, all with individual data point overlay
  • Core statistical tests — t-tests, one-way ANOVA with post-hoc comparisons, Mann-Whitney U, and Kruskal-Wallis
  • Automatic result interpretation — plain-language explanations of what your results mean
  • Complete privacy — your data is processed in your browser and never stored on our servers

How We Stay Free

The site is supported by advertising (Google AdSense). That is the entire business model: you use the tool for free, and the ads cover our hosting costs. No subscriptions, no paywalls, no selling your data.
