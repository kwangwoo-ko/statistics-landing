Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool really free?

Yes, 100% free. No hidden fees, no subscription, no credit card required. The tool is supported by advertising.

Is my data safe?

Your data is processed in your browser session and is never stored on our servers. When you close the tab, everything is gone.

What file formats are supported?

CSV (.csv) and Excel (.xlsx) files are supported. You can also compare data from multiple files at once.

Which statistical tests are available?

Student's t-test, Welch's t-test, one-way ANOVA with post-hoc tests, Mann-Whitney U test, and Kruskal-Wallis test.

Can I use this for my research paper?

Absolutely. The graphs are designed to be publication-quality and are suitable for academic papers and presentations.

This tool covers the most commonly used features of Prism — graphs and basic statistical tests — completely free. GraphPad Prism has more advanced features but costs $300–$600+ per year.
