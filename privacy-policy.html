Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how statistics.thinkflo.net ("we", "our", "the site") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and the free statistical analysis tool. By using this site, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Analysis Data Stays in Your Browser

The statistical analysis tool processes your uploaded data (CSV or Excel files) entirely within your browser session. We do not upload, store, or share your data files on our servers. When you close the browser tab, your data is gone. We never access the contents of the files you analyze.

2. Information We Collect Automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider and analytics/advertising partners may automatically collect standard technical information when you visit, including:

  • IP address (truncated or anonymized where applicable)
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring website

This information is used only for operating, securing, and improving the site, and for serving advertising.

3. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) to remember your preferences — for example, whether you have dismissed our cookie notice — and to support advertising functions. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the site.

4. Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements that keep this tool free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You may also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • For more information about how Google uses data, see Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. Your Rights (GDPR — EU/EEA Visitors)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to access personal data we hold about you
  • The right to request correction or deletion of your personal data
  • The right to object to or restrict processing, including personalized advertising
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact.thinkflo@gmail.com. You may also manage advertising consent through the cookie banner displayed on our site or through your browser settings.

6. California Residents (CCPA)

We do not sell personal information. Advertising partners may collect certain information for ad personalization as described in Section 4. California residents may opt out of personalized advertising via the links above.

7. Children's Privacy

This site is intended for a general audience, including researchers, students, and professionals, and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Because your analysis data never leaves your browser session, the risk of data exposure from using the tool is minimal. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Third-Party Links

Our site links to the free analysis tool (prism-like.onrender.com) and to other websites such as thinkflo.net. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party sites and encourage you to review their policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact.thinkflo@gmail.com.
