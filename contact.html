We welcome your questions, feedback, and suggestions. Whether you found a bug, want to request a feature, or have a question about privacy or advertising, we would love to hear from you.

What to Include

To help us respond quickly, please include:

  • What you were doing when the issue occurred
  • Your browser and operating system (for bug reports)
  • A description of the expected vs. actual behavior

Please do not send us data files — we cannot access the data you analyze in the tool, so we are unable to troubleshoot it directly.
